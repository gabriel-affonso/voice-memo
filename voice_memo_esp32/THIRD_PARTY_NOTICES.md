## Waveshare ESP32-S3-ePaper-1.54 official demo

The e-paper driver, the waveform (LUT) tables and the touch, battery and RTC
hardware facts used by the UI are taken from the official Waveshare demo for the
product this firmware targets (`ESP32-S3-Touch-ePaper-1.54`).

Source repository, at commit `9957d0f4fc7cd40d1d42880cb1b74a8d6782a6c2`:

```text
https://github.com/waveshareteam/ESP32-S3-ePaper-1.54
```

Files that were read and adapted:

```text
README.md                                             (hardware version policy)
02_Example/Arduino/09_LVGL_V8_Test/user_config.h       (EPD / touch / power pins)
02_Example/Arduino/09_LVGL_V8_Test/src/display/epaper_driver_bsp.h
02_Example/Arduino/09_LVGL_V8_Test/src/display/epaper_driver_bsp.cpp
                                                       (init sequence, LUTs, partial mode)
02_Example/Arduino/12_FT6336_Test/{12_FT6336_Test.ino,ft6336_bsp.cpp,user_config.h}
                                                       (FT6336 address, reset, registers)
02_Example/Arduino/01_ADC_Test/adc_bsp.cpp             (battery ADC channel and /2 divider)
02_Example/Arduino/08_Audio_Test/src/codec_board/board_cfg.h
                                                       ("Board: S3_ePaper_1_54" pins)
01_Arduino_Libraries/SensorLib/src/REG/PCF85063Constants.h
                                                       (RTC register map)
```

Adapted into this firmware:

```text
epd_waveform_tables.h   byte-for-byte copy of WF_Full_1IN54 / WF_PARTIAL_1IN54_0
                        (regenerate with tools/gen_epd_waveform.py)
epaper_display.cpp      port of the official SPI/GPIO/LUT sequence
touch_ft6336.cpp        port of the official I2C reads and reset timing
battery_monitor.cpp     port of the official ADC oneshot configuration
rtc_pcf85063.cpp        register map and BCD decoding
config.h                pin map copied from the official user_config.h / board_cfg.h
```

The upstream repository does not ship a root license file at that commit (only
its bundled components do: `SensorLib` is MIT (c) 2022 lewis he, `esp_codec_dev`
is the Espressif MIT license, `lvgl8`/`lvgl9` are MIT). The parts reused here are
hardware constants and the vendor's own panel command sequence for the vendor's
own board, and the provenance above is recorded so it can be re-verified or
replaced with a vendor license statement if Waveshare publishes one.
